About Us

About Melodik Wavez

Since 2015, Melodik Wavez School of Music has been inspiring children and adults across Bengaluru to discover their passion for music and the arts. With experienced trainers, structured courses and personalised guidance, we make learning enjoyable, creative and rewarding.

Our Mission:

To make quality music education accessible, enjoyable and inspiring for learners of every age.

Our Vision:

A supportive community where talent grows, discipline thrives and a lifelong love for the arts begins.

  • Performance-focused training in guitar, keyboard and more
  • Trinity-accredited founder with 30+ years in music
  • Classes for all ages, from children to adults

Mr. Shivakumar

30+ years in musicTrinity-accreditedMusician, educator, mentor

Driven by a lifelong passion for music and teaching, Mr. Shivakumar brings over 30 years of experience as a musician, educator and mentor. As the Founder of Melodik Wavez and a Trinity-accredited professional, he believes in nurturing every student's unique talent through encouragement, personalised guidance and quality music education. His vision is to create a welcoming space where students of all ages can discover their passion, grow in confidence and experience the joy of music.

Our Music Courses

Structured lessons for beginners and advanced learners, with experienced trainers and personalised guidance that build skill, confidence and stage presence at every step.

Free Demo Class

Acoustic Guitar

Learn acoustic and classical guitar with expert guidance at Melodik Wavez. Our structured classes cover Western and Indian styles, combining music theory with hands-on practice. Suitable for beginners and advanced learners, our step-by-step training builds strong fundamentals, playing techniques, confidence and stage performance skills.

Piano and Keyboard

Engaging, structured lessons in music theory, note reading, finger technique and rhythm, With personalised guidance from experienced instructors, students can progress at their own pace and explore a variety of musical styles, from Western classical to contemporary music.

Summer Classes

A fun music camp for ages 3–12 with guitars, keyboards, singing and engaging activities through a daily rotational learning format. With a playful, hands-on approach, children explore melody, harmony and rhythm while discovering their musical interests under the guidance of experienced teachers.

Learn. Create. Perform.

Flexible weekday and weekend batches, in online, offline and hybrid formats. No prior musical experience needed.
